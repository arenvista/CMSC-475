## BERT Pre-Training 
BERT is an encoder that applies two things:
1. Masked Language Modleing 
2. Next Sentence Prediction 


Consider a sequence of tokens $X$:
$$
\vec{x} = (x_n) = \underset{\text{Classifier Token}}{[x_1]} \quad \underset{\text{Sentence A}}{[x_2 \quad x_3 \quad \dots \quad x_n]} \quad \underset{Seperator}{[x_{n+1}]} \quad \underset{\text{Sentence B}}{[x_{n+2} \quad x_{n+3} \quad \dots \quad x_m]}
$$ 


BERT will makse some of the input tokens at random and try to predict those tokens in the output 

$$
\underset{Mask}{M}: \underset{Sentence}{X} \rightarrow \underset{\text{Masked Sentence}}{X'}
$$ 

Let BERT the encoder be represented as $B$
$$ B(M(x_1)) = \hat{x_2} \approx x_2 $$ 


We are training the same S and E for all queries. For all questions in this data set. 

Once the BERT is trainied we canuse it alongside the encoder / decoder

The CLS in this case the Z cls works with both sentence A and sentence B. 

Once BERT has been trainined it can do whatever input and it will find the representation for that sentence.
