# CLIP: Contrastive Learning Image 
$$ \text{Image Encoder} := \vec{I}^{n \times 1} \quad \quad \text{Text Encoder} := \vec{T}^{1 \times n} $$ 

CLIP Defines a matrix: 
$$ \vec{I} \vec{T}^T = A^{n \times n} $$ 

# Image Classification 
1. For each word in the set, create a sentence: $T = \text{`A photo of a {word}`}$
2. Encode the corresponding image to obtain its vector representation: $\vec{I}$
3. Compute the similarity between $\vec{I}$ and each $T$, and select $T$ with the highest similarity (i.e., $\operatorname{argmax}_T (I T^T)$).
